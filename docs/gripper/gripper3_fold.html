Gripper 3 fold-back

Pull the index plunger knob at the hinge and flip the kit up and back over the forearm until the bridge lands on the pad on the base (200°). The Velcro dots on the bridge and the pad hold it there; the plunger clicks into its second hole too.

  • No collisions at any angle; checked at 30° steps and stowed at 200°.
  • Stowed: the bridge rests on a sloped pad on the base, one 25 mm Velcro dot on each.
  • Stowed, the grip points up and back, and the claw tips sit about 230 mm behind the wrist ring, about 50 mm above the pipe. Still to check against the elbow in the full assembly.
