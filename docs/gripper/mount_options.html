Gripper: two mount ideas

Your ideas from 20 Oct, sketched and checked against the forearm geometry; with the wrist-joint concept for comparison.

A. Ring track: the whole kit turns round the forearm

  • Clamps go on all three pipes (replacing the two half-clips you circled), and carry a curved track: a half ring about 160 mm across (radius ~80 mm from the forearm axis) spanning ~150°, from the back of the hand round to the little-finger side.
  • The whole gripper kit (rail base, rail, gripper, handle) rides round it on rollers (608s in V-grooves), so when your hand turns, the grip and claws turn with it: about ±55° from neutral, out of your wrist's ~±75°.
  • The kit is 300 mm long, so it needs two ring tracks, one near the wrist and one near the elbow end of the pipes, on the same axis. Your hand turns about a line through the little-finger side of the wrist, not the forearm's centre, so the grip will shift ~15 mm in your hand at the ends of the turn.
  • Bending the wrist (up/down, side to side) stays locked while you hold the grip.
  • To check: turned toward the palm, the kit swings under the forearm, toward your body and thigh. The motion sweep has to clear that.

B. Hinge in the extension: fold the front away

  • A locking hinge where the handle arm meets the rail base (at the wrist ring). The front part (handle arm, grip, trigger, front half of the rail) swings round and parks flat alongside the outside of the forearm. Your hand and wrist are then completely free.
  • To use the gripper, swing it back and lock it: the grip comes into your palm between thumb and fingers.
  • Catch 1: the grip hangs 110 mm down into the palm, so swinging round a single upright hinge would sweep it through your hand. The grip needs its own fold (it folds up flat against the arm first), or the swing has to come in from the thumb side in a second motion.
  • Catch 2: the rail is split at the hinge. The carriage can only cross if the two halves line up within a few hundredths of a mm when locked, so the lock needs tapered locating pins. Until it's locked, the head must not be able to slide out (an interlock).
  • While you hold the grip, the wrist is locked again; B frees you between uses, not during them.
  • The fold must not hit the slid-back gripper (your point): stowed, the claw tips reach ~15 mm past the wrist ring and the claws cover the back of the forearm ~35 mm either side of the rail. So the hinge goes ~30 mm past the ring (ahead of the stowed claw tips), and the front part folds to the side away from your body and parks beside the stowed claws, its grip hanging down along the side of the forearm. The layout's motion check will sweep the fold with the gripper stowed and fail on any contact.
