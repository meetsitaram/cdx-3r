# CDX-3R shoulder — 2R

Two revolute joints, both **outside** the arm.

| Axis | Sheave | Where | Axis direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flexion | McMaster 3434T121 (gold) | **Lateral** of the GH joint | ML, like the elbow |
| Abduction | McMaster 3434T121 (orange) | **Posterior**, toward the pack | AP |

Elbow Bowden (cyan) **passes the comb** on the scapula pad. It does not wrap either shoulder sheave.

## Pose

90° shoulder flexion (upper arm forward). 0° abduction. Cables go **up/back to the backpack**, not down the arm.

## Physics (same r = 38.1 mm)

Lever is longer than the elbow — crate is ~0.65 m from GH.

| Crate | τ at shoulder | Cable T |
|---|---|---|
| 5 kg garage | 32 N·m | 840 N — Jagwire is tight |
| 15 kg | 96 N·m | 2500 N — 1/8 in 7×19, not PETG |
